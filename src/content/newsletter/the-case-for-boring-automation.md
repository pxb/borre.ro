## The hook

Agents are the pitch everyone is hearing. [Gartner](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-06-25-gartner-predicts-over-40-percent-of-agentic-ai-projects-will-be-canceled-by-end-of-2027) expects over 40% of agentic AI projects to be cancelled by the end of 2027, over cost, unclear value and weak risk controls.

## Two kinds of automation

A workflow follows steps you set in advance. An agent decides its own steps.

> Finding the simplest solution possible, and only increasing complexity when needed.

That's [Anthropic's advice](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents), from one of the companies building the models.

## Where agents struggle today

- About 58% success on single-step business tasks
- About 35% when the task needs a back-and-forth
- Over 83% when the task is executing a defined workflow

Source: [CRMArena-Pro, Salesforce AI Research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.18878).

## What to ask before you buy

1. Which steps are fixed, and which are AI?
2. What happens when the AI is wrong?
3. Who approves before anything goes out?
4. What does each run cost, and who sees it?
5. Can you see a log of what it did?
